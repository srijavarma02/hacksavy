## 🎥 Demo Tips

### Camera Angles
1. **Wide shot**: Full dashboard overview
2. **Close-up**: Individual components and metrics
3. **Zoom**: Highlight specific alerts and numbers
4. **Transition**: Smooth tab switching

### Pacing
- Spend 1.5 min on each major section
- Pause 2-3 seconds on important metrics
- Use cursor to guide viewer attention
- Smooth, deliberate movements

### Narration Style
- Clear, confident voice
- Technical but accessible language
- Emphasize key numbers and percentages
- Highlight automation and AI features

### Visual Effects
- Highlight boxes around important elements
- Arrows pointing to key features
- Zoom in on critical alerts
- Fade transitions between sections

### Background Music
- Subtle, modern tech music
- Low volume, don't overpower narration
- Upbeat but professional tone
